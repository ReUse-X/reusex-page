In 2026, ReUseX moves from prototype to repeatable delivery by turning scanning data into structured, decision-ready reuse datasets and strengthening the full workflow around it. We build and harden the end-to-end pipeline (capture → segmentation → inventory), add reliable metadata and validation flows with confidence scoring, and run multiple pilots to improve AI performance and reduce manual cleanup. By the end of the year, the focus shifts to productization: multi-stakeholder project workflows, export/API integrations, and standardized reporting outputs—so ReUseX can be deployed consistently across projects without compromising quality.
