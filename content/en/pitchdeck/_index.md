{{< slide >}}
## The Problem
Buildings Are a Waste Crisis

- **40%** of all global waste comes from construction and demolition
- **50%** of Europe's total material consumption is building-related
- Most reusable building components are demolished and landfilled
- Adaptive reuse is slow, expensive, and data-poor — so teams avoid it
- We are designing new buildings while destroying usable ones
{{< /slide >}}

{{< slide >}}
## The Opportunity
Reuse Is the Missing Link in the Circular Economy

- EU Renovation Wave targets **35 million buildings** renovated by 2030
- Global circular construction market projected at **€1.5 trillion** by 2030
- Carbon regulations and EPD requirements are accelerating demand
- Early-stage data on existing buildings is the #1 bottleneck for reuse
- No scalable platform exists today to close this gap
{{< /slide >}}

{{< slide >}}
## Our Solution
ReUseX — From Building to Circular Inventory in Hours

- Mobile LiDAR scanning captures full building geometry in hours, not weeks
- AI-based segmentation turns point clouds into structured 3D element libraries
- Cloud platform makes data shareable across teams, clients, and supply chains
- Circularity-ready output: quantity takeoffs, material passports, EPD-ready data
- Dramatically lowers the cost and risk of adaptive reuse decisions
{{< /slide >}}

{{< slide >}}
## How It Works
Four Steps From Building to Circular Data

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Scan" >}}Mobile LiDAR scan of the existing building — fast, low-cost, non-destructive{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Segment" >}}AI automatically identifies and classifies structural and non-structural elements{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Model" >}}Structured 3D inventory with material properties, quantities, and condition data{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Share" >}}Cloud-based platform connects designers, contractors, and material markets{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}
