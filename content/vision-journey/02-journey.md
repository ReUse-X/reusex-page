Our journey began with a simple observation: vast amounts of perfectly reusable materials are demolished every day because teams lack the data to make informed decisions. We set out to change this by combining LiDAR technology with artificial intelligence. Today, we're building the infrastructure for circular construction, one scan at a time, working with forward-thinking organizations to prove that resource reuse can scale.
